About

Ship the model, then ship the system.

I’m a software engineer focused on machine learning—developing ML models end-to-end (research, training, evaluation) and the systems that ship them. I like the stretch between notebook and production: dependable pipelines, thoughtful MLOps, and code that stays maintainable after launch.

I’m a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at CVS Health, where I drive the research and production deployment of ML models on Kubernetes—designing low-latency, horizontally scalable serving architectures so inference stays fast and dependable as traffic and use cases grow. I partner closely with platform and software engineers to harden pipelines, tighten SLOs, and ship GenAI and classical ML workloads the business can trust at enterprise scale.

Before CVS, my work lived in three places where models can’t hide behind a leaderboard: medicine (Boston Scientific, Visual DX), finance and LLM-driven analytics (Fannie Mae), and child safety—researching and building sequence models for parental-control and trust-and-safety systems at Smith Micro, where “wrong” isn’t a metric dip, it’s a human cost. Alongside the R&D, I kept one foot on the factory floor: shipping and hardening models across different production environments—from regulated pipelines and cloud services to mobile and edge—so each deployment taught me a new dialect of latency, compliance, and what “done” has to mean. Kodak Alaris added computer vision in product settings; taken together, those chapters trained me to calibrate for rigor, speed, and the person on the other side of the screen.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me on a side project when an idea won’t let go, reading or listening to something about ML, or spending downtime with family and friends.
